Bottom Dollar Privacy Policy

Effective: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bottom Dollar, developed by MT64 Labs, handles information when you use the Bottom Dollar mobile game.

Information and local game data

Bottom Dollar may store gameplay information and preferences on your device so that game progress and settings can be maintained. The developer does not sell your personal information.

Advertising

Bottom Dollar uses Google AdMob to provide advertising, including optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, ad interaction information, and related data as necessary to provide, measure, secure, and personalize advertising, subject to applicable consent choices and Google's policies.

Rewarded ads are optional. Players may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit.

Third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Information processed by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and applicable advertising controls.

Children

Bottom Dollar is not specifically directed to children. The app's store listing and advertising configuration are intended to reflect the audience selected for distribution in Google Play.

Security and retention

Gameplay data stored locally remains on the device unless removed by the user, the operating system, or an app update/reset that clears application data. Third-party service providers may retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may stop using the app or clear/uninstall it to remove locally stored application data. Advertising and privacy choices offered by your device, Google, or applicable consent interfaces may also affect how advertising data is used.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Bottom Dollar changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions about Bottom Dollar, contact MT64 Labs through the developer contact information provided on the Bottom Dollar Google Play store listing.
